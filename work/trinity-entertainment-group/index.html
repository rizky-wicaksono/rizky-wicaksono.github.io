Trinity Entertainment Group

  • Music and entertainmentIndustry
  • 2023Year
  • PR and communicationScope

Background

In 2023, I managed public relations and communication initiatives for Trinity Entertainment Group (Trinity Optima Production), one of Indonesia’s leading music and entertainment companies. My key focus was on artist visibility, media engagement, and brand storytelling, while also supporting Trinity Youth Symphony Orchestra (TRUST) in launching their first public concert, The Legends 8.

Challenge

The challenge was to maintain consistent media presence for a diverse entertainment portfolio from mainstream music artists to a classical youth orchestra, each requiring unique communication strategies. For TRUST, the task was to introduce the orchestra to a wider audience, position it as a credible youth-driven musical ensemble, and drive excitement for their debut concert.

Result

By combining strong media outreach, creative storytelling, and targeted positioning, I helped amplify both Trinity’s brand voice and TRUST’s concert presence in the public eye.

  • Planned and executed the PR strategy for TRUST’s “The Legends 8” concert, including press materials, media relations, and interview coordination.
  • Secured national media coverage across entertainment and lifestyle platforms.
  • Strengthened Trinity’s media relationships.
  • Collaborated closely with internal teams to align messaging between Trinity’s commercial artists and TRUST’s orchestral initiatives.
