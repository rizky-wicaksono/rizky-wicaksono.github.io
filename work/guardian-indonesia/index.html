Guardian Indonesia

  • Health and beauty retailIndustry
  • 2025Year
  • PR strategyScope

Background

As part of my role managing PT DFI Retail Nusantara Tbk’s communications portfolio, I also manage the Public Relations strategy for Guardian Indonesia, one of Indonesia’s leading health and beauty retailers. The main objective was to strengthen Guardian’s brand positioning as an accessible yet trusted wellness destination, while keeping the brand relevant and engaging to both loyal and new consumers.

Challenge

Guardian was transforming to refresh its brand identity, shifting from being perceived as a traditional health store to becoming a vibrant, interactive lifestyle brand. The challenge was to communicate this evolution across multiple channels, ensuring consistency between in-store experiences, media exposure, and digital engagement, while maintaining the brand’s trust and credibility in the wellness category.

Result

Through strategic storytelling and consistent brand communication, I helped redefine Guardian’s public image and increase its media visibility across health, lifestyle, and business publications.

  • Developed and executed press release and media engagement plans highlighting new store concepts, product innovation, and consumer experiences.
  • Strengthened media relations, ensuring continuous exposure aligned with the brand’s direction.
