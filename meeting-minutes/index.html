Meeting Minutes Maker

Turn a meeting transcript into minutes or a client contact report: agenda, discussion, decisions and action items. You pick what goes in; the tool only suggests.

  • Free, no sign-up
  • Nothing is uploaded
  • No AI: simple word rules you can check

1Transcript

Open .txt / .srt / .vtt file

2Meeting details

3Suggestions from the transcript

Semi-automatic. These lines were picked by simple word rules (like “deadline”, “disepakati”, “tolong”, a day or a date), not by AI. They will miss things and flag things that are not tasks. Check every line against the recording before you send the minutes.

    4Minutes

    Drag suggestions here, or use the Add buttons. Edit any text. Section titles can be renamed to match your template.

    5Preview and export

    Privacy

    Everything happens in this browser tab. The transcript is not uploaded, and this page makes no network requests while you work. Close the tab and it is gone.

    Nothing is saved unless you click “Save draft in this browser”. That keeps one draft in this browser’s local storage on this device only, until you clear it. Do not use it on a shared computer.
