Public relations engineering

PR that learns to build with AI

First tools coming soon

I can’t code, and I’m not claiming to. What I do have is enough curiosity and skill to understand how things are built, learn more, and try, with AI doing the heavy lifting.

I’m working toward a role marketing already has and PR doesn’t yet: the public relations engineer. The goal is simple: use AI to cut the repetitive parts of PR, such as media monitoring, first drafts, list-building and reporting, so more time goes to strategy and relationships.

  • Learning how AI tools and code work, enough to understand and try them
  • Turning my PR playbooks into small, reusable tools, with AI’s help
  • Writing about what works and what doesn’t

Open to collaborations on PR and AI, speaking, and comms teams that want to automate the boring parts.
