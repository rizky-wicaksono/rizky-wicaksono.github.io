Background

In 2024, I worked on a campaign for DAIKIN Indonesia, supporting the launch of its new factory and first line of locally produced products. The initiative was part of DAIKIN’s broader mission toward carbon neutrality and sustainability. To bring this vision to life, I developed the strategic communication direction for the #KeputusanYangTepat campaign.

Challenge

The main challenge was to craft a strong narrative that positioned DAIKIN as a leader in sustainable innovation, not just in product quality but also in long-term environmental commitment. This required a 360-degree communication approach, combining branding, influencer engagement, and media relations to deliver consistent messaging across all touchpoints.

Result

Through this campaign, I helped reinforce DAIKIN’s brand positioning as a trusted, sustainable air solution provider. The initiative successfully built positive media traction, strengthened consumer awareness, and aligned with DAIKIN’s FY24 product strategy, with the campaign continuing through 2025 to support long-term brand growth and innovation.

  • Secured 334+ media coverages, including multiple tier-1 outlets.
  • Organized 3 press conferences and 1 exclusive media interview to amplify key messages.
  • Reached over 11M+ total views across online platforms through KOL collaborations.
