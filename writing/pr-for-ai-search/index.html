PR for AI search: how to get your brand mentioned in AI answers

By Rizky Wicaksono · 25 November 2026 · 5 min read

In short

AI search tools tend to rely on sources that look credible and clear. PR helps by earning coverage on credible third-party sites, keeping facts about your brand consistent everywhere, and publishing clear answers that are easy to quote. Nobody outside the platforms knows the exact rules, so treat these as good practice, not guarantees.

What GEO means

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making your brand and content easier for AI tools to find, understand and cite when they answer a question. It overlaps strongly with SEO and with PR. It is a young field, and platforms change often, so avoid anyone who promises guaranteed results.

Earn coverage on credible third-party sites

AI answers tend to lean on sources that look independent and trustworthy: news outlets, industry media, official pages. Coverage you earn through PR is exactly that kind of source. A mention in a respected outlet carries more weight than a statement on your own website.

Keep your facts consistent everywhere

Use the same name spelling, job title, description and key facts across your website, LinkedIn, press releases and boilerplates. When sources agree, it is easier for both people and machines to be sure who you are and what you do. Contradictions create confusion and wrong answers.

Write answers that are easy to quote

  • Put the direct answer first, then the detail.
  • Use clear headings that match real questions.
  • Add a short summary at the top and a visible FAQ at the bottom.
  • Show who wrote it and when it was published or updated.

Get the technical basics right

Make sure your pages can be crawled, have unique titles and descriptions, are listed in a sitemap, and use structured data such as Person or Article markup where it fits. Many AI search tools draw on regular web search results, so good SEO still helps.

Use your own profiles carefully

Keep your LinkedIn and other professional profiles complete and consistent, and link them to your website. Do not create self-promotional pages on platforms such as Wikipedia; they have notability rules and will remove promotional content.

Check what AI tools say about you

Every month or two, ask a few AI tools questions about your brand or your name and note what they say and which sources they cite. If something is wrong, find the source it came from and fix or update that page. Over time, this shows you which sources matter.

Frequently asked questions

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. They overlap. Many AI search tools use web search results, so clear, credible, well-structured content helps in both.

Can I pay to be mentioned in AI answers?

There is no reliable way to buy it. Be wary of anyone who sells guaranteed AI mentions.

How long does it take to see results?

It depends on how fast new coverage and pages are indexed and picked up. Think in months, not days.
