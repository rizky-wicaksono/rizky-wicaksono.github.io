PT DFI Retail Nusantara Tbk

  • RetailIndustry
  • 2025Year
  • PR and communication strategyScope

Background

Since 2025, I have been responsible for managing public relations and communication strategies for PT DFI Retail Nusantara Tbk, overseeing its two flagship retail brands, Guardian Indonesia and IKEA Indonesia. My focus has been on strengthening brand reputation, amplifying visibility, and maintaining consistent communication across both brands.

Challenge

When I joined, PT DFI Retail Nusantara Tbk had just transitioned from its previous identity as PT Hero Supermarket Tbk, while still retaining the stock code HERO. The key challenge was to build public and media awareness of the new corporate identity, not by telling the story directly, but by doing so through its consumer-facing brands, Guardian and IKEA. This required a strategic balance: reinforcing the brands’ own narratives while subtly communicating the parent company’s transformation and long-term vision.

Result

Through integrated communication planning and consistent brand storytelling, I helped strengthen DFI’s visibility and credibility during its transition period.

  • Strengthened media relations and press outreach, resulting in continuous tier-1 coverage for DFI, Guardian and IKEA’s initiatives.
  • Supported DFI’s corporate rebranding efforts by ensuring all communication materials, releases, and campaign narratives aligned with the company’s refreshed positioning.
